There's no approved cure for ME/CFS yet. But some treatments help some people with some symptoms, and some things reliably make it worse. This section sets out the evidence honestly so you and your doctor can decide what's worth trying.

{% callout "warn", "Please read" %}
Nothing here is medical advice. **Please make any treatment or supplement decision with a doctor who knows your history.** Doses mentioned are those used in studies or common practice, not recommendations for you. Supplements and off-label drugs can interact with other medications.
{% endcallout %}
