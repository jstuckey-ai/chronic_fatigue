There's no cure for ME/CFS yet. But there's a lot you can do to **stop it getting worse** and to give your body the best chance of improving. The most important of these is [pacing](/living/pacing/).

Read [Pacing](/living/pacing/). Staying inside your energy limits is the most widely shared advice across recent guidelines and patient organisations.
