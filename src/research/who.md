## The landscape

No major pharmaceutical company is developing an ME/CFS drug. The work is being done by **small biotech companies, research institutes and patient-funded charities**. Much of the recent progress has come via **Long COVID**, which has far more patients and funding. The likeliest path to a treatment is that a drug gets approved for Long COVID first and is then used for ME/CFS.

## Research centres

| Organisation | Focus | Status (Sept 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **Griffith University NCNED** (Gold Coast, Australia) | TRPM3 ion channel research; LDN trials; brain waste clearance | TreatMELC LDN trial starting; mechanism studies recruiting |
| **Deakin University / Barwon Health** (Geelong, Australia) | TRI-ME trimetazidine trial | Recruiting in Victoria |
| **Charité** (Berlin, Germany) | Immunoadsorption, HBOT, inebilizumab (PIONEER), aripiprazole (PAIS-AriSE), rehab | The largest clinical trial hub in the field; German government funded |
| **Haukeland University Hospital** (Bergen, Norway) | Daratumumab (RESETME) | 66-patient trial under way, closed to new applicants |
| **Stanford** (US) | PET imaging; low-dose aripiprazole clinic data | Research |
| **Johns Hopkins** (US) | Blood-brain barrier study | Under way |
| **Brigham & Women's Hospital** (US) | Autonomic and blood flow testing | Research |
| **University of Edinburgh / DecodeME** (UK) | Genetics; blood biomarkers | Next phase: Sequence ME & Long Covid |

## Companies

| Company | Treatment | Status (Sept 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| **BioVie** | Bezisterim (oral anti-inflammatory that reaches the brain) | Phase 2 Long COVID results in Sept 2026; Phase 3 planned |
| **Simmaron Research** (non-profit) | Low-dose rapamycin{% ref "ruan-2025" %} | Placebo-controlled follow-up planned for mid-2027 |
| **APTA Therapeutics** (Germany) | Rovunaptabin (formerly BC007), an autoantibody neutraliser{% ref "apta-2025", "hohberger-2025" %} | Acquired Berlin Cures' assets after the company's insolvency; new Long COVID trial planned in Coimbra, Portugal |
| **Mitodicure** (Germany) | MDC002, targeting muscle energy and PEM{% ref "mitodicure" %} | Preclinical; no human trials yet |
| **AIM ImmunoTech** | Ampligen (rintatolimod), IV | 30+ years of development; not approved anywhere for ME/CFS |
| **Fareon** (Mount Sinai spin-out) | Wearable magnetic device | Long COVID randomised pilot published Sept 2026; invite-only follow-up pilot; larger trial planned for early 2027 |

## Funders

- **Solve M.E.** (US): funds research including Simmaron's rapamycin work{% ref "solve-simmaron-2025" %}
- **Open Medicine Foundation** (US/international): funds the LIFT trial (Boston), the StudyME registry and more
- **ME/CFS Research Foundation** (Germany): €2.4 million to seven projects in its 2026 funding round{% ref "mecfs-rf-funding-2026" %}
- **ME Research UK** and the **ME Association** (UK)
- **Stafford Fox Medical Research Foundation** (Australia): $6.4 million over five years to Griffith NCNED, including its LDN trials{% ref "griffith-stafford-fox-2023" %}
- **NHMRC** (Australia): funds TRI-ME, and is developing the first Australian ME/CFS clinical guideline with $1.1 million from the Federal Government{% ref "butler-2024", "nhmrc-guideline-dev" %}
- **NIH RECOVER-TLC** (US): Long COVID trials
- **Canadian Institutes of Health Research**
