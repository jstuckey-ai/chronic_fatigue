ME/CFS research has grown quickly since COVID-19, as Long COVID brought new attention and funding. This section tracks what's happening now: trials looking for participants, where researchers are meeting, and what recent studies have (and haven't) found.

{% callout "tip", "Taking part in research" %}
Joining a study is one of the most direct ways to push the science forward, and some studies include free testing, such as immune panels or MRI scans. See [how joining a trial works](/research/trials/#how-to-join).
{% endcallout %}
