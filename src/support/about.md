## Why this site exists

chronicfatigue.support was created by {{ site.author }} to be a **one-stop place** for people with ME/CFS, and the people who care for them, to find clear, honest, current information. It covers what the illness is, what helps, what the research is showing, and which trials are looking for participants.

ME/CFS research is scattered across journals, conference talks, trial registries and patient forums. Much of it is written for specialists, and a lot of what reaches patients is hype. We try to bring it together in plain language and be upfront about how strong the evidence is.

## How we write

- **Plain language first.** Many readers have brain fog, so pages start with a short "In brief" summary, and headings let you jump to what you need.
- **Honest about evidence.** Every treatment claim is labelled with the kind of study behind it (see below).
- **Negative results included.** Knowing what *didn't* work saves people time, money and energy.
- **Dated.** Each page shows when it was last reviewed. Trial details were checked against official registries on the date shown.

## Medical disclaimer

{% callout "warn" %}
**This site provides general information only. It is not medical advice** and isn't a substitute for care from a qualified health professional who knows your history. Please don't start, stop or change any medication, supplement or treatment based on this site without talking to your doctor.

Doses mentioned are those used in research or common clinical practice and are included to support informed conversations with your doctor. They are not recommendations for any individual.

Clinical trial information is provided to help you find studies. We are not affiliated with any trial listed, and eligibility is decided by each study team. Always check the official registry or study website for current details.

We don't sell anything, take advertising, or receive payment from any company, clinic or trial mentioned.
{% endcallout %}

## Accessibility

- Use **Comfort** (top of every page) to make text larger, switch to dim colours, or turn on **calm mode** (softer contrast, no animation).
- The site works with screen readers and keyboard navigation, and can be printed cleanly.
- The body font, Atkinson Hyperlegible, was designed for readers with low vision.
