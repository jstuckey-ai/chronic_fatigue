- **Emerge Australia nurse support & telehealth:** [1800 865 321](tel:1800865321) (press 1), Monday–Friday 9am–4:30pm AEDT. Free.
- **Lifeline:** [13 11 14](tel:131114), 24 hours, for crisis support
- **13YARN:** [13 92 76](tel:139276), 24 hours, for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people
- **Carer Gateway:** [1800 422 737](tel:1800422737) for carers
- **Emergency:** 000
